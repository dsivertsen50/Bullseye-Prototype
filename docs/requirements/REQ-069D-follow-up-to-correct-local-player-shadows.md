The new first-person body illusion is substantially better, but there is one rendering issue to correct.

Currently, the local player's shadow appears to be generated by the dedicated `FPBody` and `FPArms`, so the shadow consists mainly of arms and legs with no complete torso/head.

That is not the intended behavior.

## Desired Shadow Architecture

For the owning/local player:

```text
FPBody
- visible to first-person player
- Cast Shadows = OFF

FPArms
- visible to first-person player
- Cast Shadows = OFF

Full World StickMan
- not directly visible in the owner's first-person view
- remains fully animated
- Cast Shadows = ON / Shadows Only
```

The player's shadow should therefore be generated by the **complete third-person/world character**, not by the dedicated first-person meshes.

The local player should see a natural complete-character shadow containing:

* head
* torso
* arms
* legs
* equipped world-view weapon where appropriate

while still directly seeing only the FPBody/FPArms first-person presentation.

Please inspect how the full world renderer is currently hidden for the owning client.

If it is being disabled with something equivalent to:

```text
renderer.enabled = false
```

do not use that approach if it prevents shadow casting.

Instead, where practical, use Unity's `ShadowCastingMode.ShadowsOnly` on the owner's full world-body `SkinnedMeshRenderer`.

Remote player instances should continue rendering their complete world character normally with standard shadow casting.

Also ensure the dedicated FPBody and FPArms do not cast their own shadows, otherwise duplicate/misaligned limb shadows may occur.

Do not change networking or animation behavior for this fix. This should be a local rendering/presentation correction only.
